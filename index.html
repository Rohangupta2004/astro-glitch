MISSION MANUAL 🤖⚡🌌

Objective: Guide "Byte" the Cyber-Bot across 24 dangerous security sectors to defeat Rogue AI Nexus-9, rescue Dr. Maya Lin's memory shards, and reach the Quantum Stargate!


Movement & Cyber-Vehicle Controls:

  • A / D or Arrow Keys: Move Left / Right (or Steer Rover)
  • W / Space: Jump (or Rover Nitro Boost / Thruster Hop)
  • [E]: Mount / Dismount Apex Cyber-Rover (near vehicle) or Chronos Slow (on foot)
  • [U]: Open Quantum Workshop to upgrade abilities with collected Nanites!

Obtainables & Progression:

  • 💎 Dr. Maya Lin's Memory Shards: 1 hidden per sector! Unlocks emotional neural diary logs of Byte's creator.
  • ⚡ Nanite Cores: Collect golden nanites scattered across sectors to purchase 6 branches of upgrades in the Workshop!
  • 🏎️ Apex Cyber-Rover: High-speed armored vehicle! Safely drives over spikes, rams breakables, and jumps across giant voids with nitro!

Quantum & Superhero Abilities:

  • [SHIFT] / [1]: Phase Shift – Phase through holographic barriers, security drones, and plasma traps.
  • [E] / [2]: Chronos Slow – Slow down world time to weave through falling bulkheads and lasers.
  • [Q] / [F] / [3]: Polarity Invert – Walk on ceilings to bypass bottomless plasma pits.
  • [R] / [4]: Quantum Rewind – Rewind time 2.5 seconds to restore your state!
  • [T] / [5]: Cosmic Wormhole – Sub-space teleport across massive voids and singularities!
  • [Z] / [6]: Heat Vision 🔥 – Blasts optic laser beams! Melts titanium blast walls and sentries!
  • [X] / [7]: Aegis Shield 🛡️ – Invincible forcefield that absorbs 1 fatal hit!
  • [C] / [V] / [8]: Thunder Slam 💥 – Air dive to shatter cracked decks and stun enemies!
